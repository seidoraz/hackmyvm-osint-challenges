# HackMyVM OSINT - Challenge 003

## 📝 Challenge Information

* **Category:** OSINT
* **Challenge Name:** OSINT 003
* **Objective:** Identify the person or the mural depicted in the provided image.
* **Flag Format:** `HMV{namelastname}` (e.g., `HMV{johnwick}`)

## 🔍 Investigation Methodology

### Step 1: Initial Image Analysis
The challenge provided an image of a graffiti/mural on a concrete wall depicting a person with their face partially covered by a red bandana or cloth. 

### Step 2: Reverse Image Search
To identify the subject, I utilized **Google Reverse Image Search** (Google Lens) by uploading the target image.

<img width="1604" height="1362" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/73f9ebe5-6ecc-414b-b51d-1eb6ca56fe34" />


### Step 3: Finding the Match
The visual search successfully matched the mural to a well-known piece of street art. The search results pointed directly to **Gata Cattana** (Ana Isabel García Llorente), a famous Spanish rapper and poet known for her political and feminist themes, often depicted in this specific graffiti style.

---

## 🏁 Flag Retrieval

Based on the flag format `HMV{namelastname}`, the final flag was constructed using her artistic name:

**Flag:** `HMV{gatacattana}`
